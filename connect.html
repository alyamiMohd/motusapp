Connect your apps

Connect once and Motus keeps itself up to date, or import a file whenever you like. There's no Motus account and no Motus server: what you see is kept on this device.

Automatic

Set it up once and Motus keeps itself up to date: Apple Health from your iPhone every day (with MyFitnessPal and every other app that writes to Health), and Hevy every few hours. Your own free GitHub account does the work, and your data is encrypted with a key only you have.

Setup, about ten minutes
  1. Make your copy. Sign in to GitHub (free), then create your copy of motus-sync. Name it motus-sync and keep it Public; your data will be encrypted.
  2. Your key.
    Add it to your copy as a secret named MOTUS_KEY: open Secrets → New repository secret. It's saved on this device too.
  3. Hevy (optional, Hevy Pro): add a second secret, HEVY_API_KEY, with your key from hevy.com → Settings → Developer.
  4. Turn on GitHub Pages: open Pages → Source: GitHub Actions.
  5. First run: open Motus sync → Run workflow. Wait for the green tick, about a minute.
  7. On your iPhone, for Apple Health: create a phone token. Under Repository access choose Only select repositories and pick your copy; the rest is filled in. Copy the token, then add the Motus Shortcut and a daily automation.

Type your copy's name in step 1 and the links light up.

Or import once

Apple Health

Steps, sleep, food energy and weight. Apps that write to Health, like MyFitnessPal or a smart scale, come along with it.

  1. On your iPhone, open Health and tap your picture at the top right.
  2. Tap Export All Health Data, then Export. It can take a few minutes.
  3. Choose Save to Files.
  4. Come back here and choose that export.zip.

Big exports take a minute and are read on your phone, never uploaded. JSON files from the Health Exporter app work too.

Hevy

Workouts, estimated 1RM and ratings.

With your API key (Hevy Pro)

  1. Sign in at hevy.com and open Settings → Developer.
  2. Copy your API key and paste it here.

The key goes only from this browser to Hevy, to read your workouts. Motus has no server to send it to.

Or with an export (any Hevy account)

  1. In the Hevy app: Profile → Settings → Export & Import Data → Export Workouts.
  2. Save the CSV, then choose it here.

Strong

Workouts, estimated 1RM and ratings.

  1. In Strong: Settings → Export Strong Data.
  2. Save the CSV, then choose it here.

Most Strong files say their unit; this is used only when they don't.

MyFitnessPal

Calories eaten and weight.

  1. With Premium, ask for your data export on myfitnesspal.com (Settings → Export data). It arrives as a zip.
  2. Choose the zip here, or the Nutrition Summary and Measurement Summary files inside it.

No Premium? Connect MyFitnessPal to Apple Health in its settings, then import Apple Health above.

Any other app

Google Fit, Health Connect, Fitbit, Garmin, Samsung Health, Oura, Withings… There's no direct import for these yet. Put your daily numbers in a spreadsheet, one row per day, and save it as CSV.

Columns: date (like 2026-10-25), sleep_hours, steps, calories, weight_kg or weight_lb, workouts, workout_minutes. Leave out what you don't track.

Enter by hand

Add or fix a single day. What you type here wins over imported numbers.

Your data

    Privacy

    Motus has no accounts and no server. Files you import are read by this page in your browser and saved in this browser's storage on this device; they aren't uploaded anywhere. With Automatic, your phone and Hevy send your numbers to your own GitHub copy; GitHub handles them while the job runs and keeps only a file encrypted with your key, which Motus opens here. Nothing is sold or shared, and there's no tracking or cookies. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees your IP address when you load them. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes everything here; another browser or device starts empty, so use a backup to move.
