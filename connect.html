Connect your apps

Motus reads your exports right here in your browser and keeps them on this device. No account, no server, nothing uploaded.

Apple Health

Steps, sleep, food energy and weight. Apps that write to Health, like MyFitnessPal or a smart scale, come along with it.

  1. On your iPhone, open Health and tap your picture at the top right.
  2. Tap Export All Health Data, then Export. It can take a few minutes.
  3. Choose Save to Files.
  4. Come back here and choose that export.zip.

Big exports take a minute and are read on your phone, never uploaded. JSON files from the Health Exporter app work too.

Hevy

Workouts, estimated 1RM and ratings.

With your API key (Hevy Pro)

  1. Sign in at hevy.com and open Settings → Developer.
  2. Copy your API key and paste it here.

The key goes only from this browser to Hevy, to read your workouts. Motus has no server to send it to.

Or with an export (any Hevy account)

  1. In the Hevy app: Profile → Settings → Export & Import Data → Export Workouts.
  2. Save the CSV, then choose it here.

Strong

Workouts, estimated 1RM and ratings.

  1. In Strong: Settings → Export Strong Data.
  2. Save the CSV, then choose it here.

Most Strong files say their unit; this is used only when they don't.

MyFitnessPal

Calories eaten and weight.

  1. With Premium, ask for your data export on myfitnesspal.com (Settings → Export data). It arrives as a zip.
  2. Choose the zip here, or the Nutrition Summary and Measurement Summary files inside it.

No Premium? Connect MyFitnessPal to Apple Health in its settings, then import Apple Health above.

Any other app

Google Fit, Health Connect, Fitbit, Garmin, Samsung Health, Oura, Withings… There's no direct import for these yet. Put your daily numbers in a spreadsheet, one row per day, and save it as CSV.

Columns: date (like 2026-10-25), sleep_hours, steps, calories, weight_kg or weight_lb, workouts, workout_minutes. Leave out what you don't track.

Enter by hand

Add or fix a single day. What you type here wins over imported numbers.

Your data

    Privacy

    Motus has no accounts and no server. Your files are read by this page in your browser and saved in this browser's storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded, sold or shared, and there's no tracking or cookies. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees your IP address when you load them. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes everything here; another browser or device starts empty, so use a backup to move.
