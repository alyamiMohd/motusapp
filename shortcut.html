The Motus Shortcut

Your iPhone sends the last two days of Apple Health to your Motus copy every day, by itself. MyFitnessPal and every other app that writes to Health come along. You make it once; it takes about five minutes.

You need

  1. Your copy of motus-sync set up (Connect → Automatic, steps 1–6).
  2. Your phone token from step 7. It starts with github_pat_.
  3. The Health Exporter & Shortcuts app from the App Store. Open it once and let it read Steps, Sleep, Dietary Energy, Active Energy and Weight.

Make it

  1. Open Shortcuts, tap +, and name the new shortcut Motus.
  2. Add the action Export Health Data (from Health Exporter). Choose the last 2 days, totals per day, as JSON.
  3. Add Base64 Encode. If it shows Line Breaks, choose None.
  4. Add Get Contents of URL and fill it in:
    • URL https://api.github.com/repos/YOUR-NAME/motus-sync/dispatches
    • Method POST
    • Header Authorization = Bearer followed by your phone token
    • Header Accept = application/vnd.github+json
    • Request Body JSON, with two fields:
      event_type (Text) = health
      client_payload (Dictionary), and inside it health_b64 (Text) = the Base64 Encoded result from step 3
  5. Tap ▶ to try it. Nothing appears on the phone (GitHub answers with an empty reply). Within a few seconds your copy's Actions shows a new Motus sync run; when it's green, open Motus and the numbers are there.

Make it run by itself

  1. In Shortcuts, open Automation and tap +.
  2. Choose Time of Day, pick a time after you usually wake up, and Daily.
  3. Choose Run Immediately, then the Motus shortcut.
  4. Optional: a second one in the evening, so today's steps show before bed.

Each run sends yesterday and today, so a missed day catches up on the next run. The phone token lasts a year at most; when it runs out, create a new one (Connect → Automatic, step 7) and paste it into the Shortcut.
