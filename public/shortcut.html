The Motus Shortcut

Add it in one tap: Add the Motus Shortcut

Then go to Motus → Connect → Finish under Apple Health. It uses only Apple's own Shortcuts actions, no other app.

What it does

  • Reads Steps, Active Energy, Dietary Energy, Weight and Sleep from Apple Health. Nothing else: no heart rate, workouts, medical records or location, and it never changes anything in Health.
  • First run (from Finish): the last 30 days, to fill your charts. It can take a minute on a phone with lots of data.
  • Every run after (the daily automation, or a tap): the last 2 days, and a week of food and weight to catch late entries.
  • iPhone and Watch Steps and energy go sample by sample with their source, so Motus can count the busiest device each day instead of adding the two together.
  • Where it sends Only to your Motus account. Finish gives it your account's address and a private token, kept in a small file (motus.txt) in the Shortcuts folder. Disconnect in Motus cancels the token.

Make it run by itself

  1. In Shortcuts, open Automation and tap +.
  2. Choose Time of Day, a morning time, Daily.
  3. Choose Run Immediately, then the Motus shortcut.

If the Shortcut ever says it can't read a type, open the Health app → your picture → Apps → Shortcuts and turn that type on.

How it's made

The Shortcut is generated from tools/shortcut/build.mjs, so every action can be read there.
