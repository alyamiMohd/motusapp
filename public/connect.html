Apple HealthiPhone: steps, sleep, weight, food, energy

Open this page on your iPhone to connect Apple Health.

  1. Add the Motus Shortcut
    Tap Add Shortcut, then come back.

  2. Turn everything on, tap Allow. Keep Shortcuts open until it brings you back (about a minute).
  3. Done. Motus updates Health each time you open it. Optional daily update: Shortcuts → Automation → + → Time of Day → Daily → Run Immediately → Motus.

Health ConnectAndroid: Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, HUAWEI…
  1. Get HC Webhook
    Free on Google Play.

  2. Nothing to type.
  3. Import it: HC Webhook → ⋮ → Settings backup → Import → motus-hc-webhook.json, then Grant permissions.
  4. Done. Your phone sends data every hour.
