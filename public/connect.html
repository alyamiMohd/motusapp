Connect

Connect your apps once and Motus keeps itself up to date.

Sign in to connect your apps

With your email. There's no password: we send you a 6-digit code. New here? The same code makes your account.

Connecting apps needs a Motus account. Open Motus here to sign in.

Integrations

Apple HealthiPhone: steps, sleep, weight, food, energy

Open this page on your iPhone to connect Apple Health.

  1. Add the Motus Shortcut
    Tap Add Shortcut, then come back here.

  2. Your iPhone asks to let Motus read Health: turn everything on and tap Allow. This first run brings in your last 30 days: keep Shortcuts open, with the screen on, until it brings you back here (about a minute).
  3. That's it. Whenever you open Motus on this iPhone and Health hasn't been read for 12 hours, Motus runs the Shortcut and comes straight back. Optional: to keep it current even on days you don't open Motus, add a daily automation (Apple only lets you do this yourself): Shortcuts → Automation → + → Time of Day → a morning time, Daily → Run Immediately → Next → Motus.

Health ConnectAndroid: Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, HUAWEI…
  1. Get HC Webhook
    A free app on Google Play that sends Health Connect's numbers to Motus every hour. It's not made by Motus. Health Connect itself is built into Android 14 and later (get it here on older phones).

  2. It holds Motus's address and your private key, so there's nothing to type.
  3. Import it in HC Webhook: open HC Webhook → ⋮ → Settings backup → Import → choose motus-hc-webhook.json (in Downloads). Then tap Grant permissions and allow steps, active calories, nutrition, weight and sleep.
  4. That's it. Your phone sends the last 2 days every hour. Make sure the app you track with (Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, HUAWEI Health…) shares with Health Connect in its own settings.

MyFitnessPalFood, through Apple Health or Health Connect

MyFitnessPal shares your food with your phone's health app, and Motus reads it from there.

  1. In MyFitnessPal's settings, connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) and let it write nutrition.
  2. Connect Apple Health or Health Connect above.

Your numbers are kept in your Motus account so they arrive by themselves and show on any of your devices. Nothing is sold or shared, and there's no tracking or advertising. You can delete everything from your profile.
