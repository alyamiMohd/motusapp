Hevy

Your workouts, ratings and estimated 1RM, kept up to date by themselves. Needs Hevy Pro.

  1. On the Hevy page that just opened, copy your API key (if there isn't one, create it).
  2. Come back here and tap Paste key.

Apple Health

Steps, sleep, weight and food energy from your iPhone, every day. MyFitnessPal and other apps that write to Health come with it.

Open this page on your iPhone to connect Apple Health.

  1. Add the Motus Shortcut
    Tap Add Shortcut, then come back here.

  2. Your iPhone asks to let Motus read Health: turn everything on and tap Allow. This first run brings in your last 30 days, so it can take a minute.
  3. Make it daily. iOS asks you to do this one yourself: in Shortcuts → Automation → + → Time of Day → a morning time, Daily → Run Immediately → Next → Motus.

MyFitnessPal

Comes in through Apple Health: in MyFitnessPal's settings, connect Apple Health and let it write your nutrition. Then connect Apple Health above.

Or import a file

Apple Health export, Hevy or Strong CSV, MyFitnessPal export, a spreadsheet, or a day by hand

Apple Health export

Your whole history at once: steps, sleep, food energy and weight.

  1. On your iPhone, open Health and tap your picture at the top right.
  2. Tap Export All Health Data, then Export. It can take a few minutes.
  3. Choose Save to Files.
  4. Come back here and choose that export.zip.

Big exports take a minute and are read on your phone. JSON files from the Health Exporter app work too.

Hevy export

For any Hevy account, Pro or not.

  1. In the Hevy app: Profile → Settings → Export & Import Data → Export Workouts.
  2. Save the CSV, then choose it here.

Strong

Workouts, estimated 1RM and ratings. Strong has no way to connect directly, so it comes as a file.

  1. In Strong: Settings → Export Strong Data.
  2. Save the CSV, then choose it here.

Most Strong files say their unit; this is used only when they don't.

MyFitnessPal export

Calories eaten and weight, from MyFitnessPal Premium's data export.

  1. Ask for your data export on myfitnesspal.com (Settings → Export data). It arrives as a zip.
  2. Choose the zip here, or the Nutrition Summary and Measurement Summary files inside it.

Any other app

Google Fit, Health Connect, Fitbit, Garmin, Samsung Health, Oura, Withings… There's no direct connection for these yet. Put your daily numbers in a spreadsheet, one row per day, and save it as CSV.

Columns: date (like 2026-10-25), sleep_hours, steps, calories, weight_kg or weight_lb, workouts, workout_minutes. Leave out what you don't track.

Your data

    Privacy

    Signed in, your numbers are kept in your Motus account so they can arrive by themselves and show on any of your devices. Hevy's key is stored encrypted and used only to read your workouts; the Motus Shortcut sends only steps, sleep, weight and energy. Nothing is sold or shared, and there's no tracking or advertising. Without an account, files you import stay in this browser on this device. Deleting your account removes everything from Motus at once.
