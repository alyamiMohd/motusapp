How ratings work

Each exercise's best set is turned into an estimated one-rep max (weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30), sets of 12 reps or fewer, warm-ups left out). A workout compares each exercise with your average of its last four sessions: 7 is a normal day, up to 10 when you beat it by 10%, never below 3. Exercises you haven't done at least twice before are listed but not rated. The 4-week rating mixes progress (70%) with how often you trained against your goal (30%).
